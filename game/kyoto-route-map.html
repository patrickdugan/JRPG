Nagasaki to Kyoto (Miyako)

Northern Road selected

The western Japan map offers a direct sea passage, a northern overland road, and a southern passage through Shikoku. All reach Kyoto.

Direct Sea Passage sails north from Nagasaki through Hirado and Kanmon, crosses the Seto Inland Sea, lands at Sakai, and finishes overland. Northern Road crosses northern Kyushu and western Honshu. Southern Passage turns through Shimabara, crosses Bungo, and traverses Shikoku and Awaji before climbing to inland Kyoto.
