The Road to Edo map has three branches from Hoshigawa: Cedar Ridge, Witness Road, and Lantern Coast. All three converge at Edo.

Cedar Ridge climbs northeast through Takamine and Kagura. Witness Road travels east through Kozui and Hushroad. Lantern Coast bends southeast through Sodegaura and Nagi before turning north into Edo.
