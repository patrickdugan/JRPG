# Miyo Senda — Fourfold Weather Scholar

## Canonical identity

- Public name: Miyo Senda
- Baptismal family-book entry: Inês Avelar
- Heritage: three-quarters Japanese and one-quarter Portuguese
- Family: literal granddaughter of Father Mateus Avelar through his half-Japanese daughter and the Japanese Senda household
- Join point: `c7-01-decision-map-table`, permanent thereafter
- Party function: mobile Arcane specialist and weather-lane controller

Miyo is Japanese in upbringing, language, clothing, working practice, and public identity. “Inês Avelar” is a private archival fact, not an alternate costume, a foreign-facing default, or a name other characters may impose on her. Mateus uses it once when surprised; Miyo corrects him immediately.

Her existence does not redeem Mateus. She can recognize useful change in him without serving as evidence that he deserves trust or forgiveness. Her Chapter 7 boundary is explicit: her grandfather is not her verdict, and she is not proof that he has changed.

## Chronology and introduction

Mateus had a half-Japanese daughter before abandoning the family routes he later betrayed. That daughter entered the Senda household and raised Miyo there. The exact ages remain deliberately unstated until the master chronology fixes Mateus's human and vampiric years; the relationship itself is literal, not honorary or symbolic.

Miyo enters at the Chapter 7 map table carrying a corrected enemy chain schedule. Her first scene establishes four facts in order:

1. Her field information is materially useful before her ancestry is discussed.
2. She uses “Miyo Senda” and corrects Mateus's abandoned family-book name.
3. The Senda household, not Mateus, raised her.
4. Her copy has independent provenance: the signed original remains with the ferry council.

She joins because the Hushroad rescue intersects her own work. She does not join to reconcile with Mateus.

## Fourfold weather calculus

Miyo's practice combines Japanese household teaching, coastal observation, navigation tables, and her own repeatable experiments. It is an original setting-specific discipline with its own tool, gesture language, and combat timings.

| Technique | Delivery / essence | Recovery | Tactical purpose |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Cinder Glyph | Arcane / Ember | 2 pulses | Reliable Ember pressure at range 4 |
| White Current | Arcane / Frost | 2 pulses | Longer-range Frost lane control |
| Thunder Thread | Arcane / Storm | 3 pulses | High-power commitment with a punishable recovery window |
| Crosswind Step | Arcane / neutral | 1 pulse | Two-space reposition while preserving free movement |

One recovery pulse is 800 ms before level and equipment modifiers. Technique animation and recovery are separate contracts: the damage or movement event fires at its authored key, while the recovery timer continues afterward. Crosswind Step is utility, not teleportation through blocked or occupied cells.

Starting equipment is the Fourfold Weather Ruler, River-Silk Robe, and Storm-Kite Toggle. Her starting vow is Measured Breath. The ruler is folding bamboo with brass joints for pressure, current, temperature, and charge; it is an instrument and close-range guard, not a staff copied from another character.

## Late-campaign dramatic function

- Chapter 7: corrects the chain table, creates a cold-current route with Kiku, and rejects being used as Mateus's moral verdict.
- Chapter 8: completes the outer-court role grammar by changing declared weather lanes.
- Chapter 9: enters Kurohana as the seventh party member.
- Blood Conservatory: Kurozane tries to treat her Japanese name and baptismal record as two handles on one person. Miyo refuses the classification before he presents the six existing tailored temptations. The six choice flags remain mechanically bounded; her refusal is canonical and unconditional.
- Ending: contributes weather records and shared route practice rather than inheriting authority from either family line.
