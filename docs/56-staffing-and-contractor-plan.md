## 1. Purpose and Production Posture

*Bells of the Black Chrysanthemum* is past preproduction and systems prototyping. Its 80% vision and technical foundation are locked and deterministically verified in-repository:

- **Systems, engine, and narrative spine:** 11 chapters, 60 canonical campaign scenes, 40 Storyworld scenes across 12 bounded decision clusters (76–77 played scenes per selected Salt/Ash/Paper route), 48 collision-authored levels, 23 canonical encounters across 20 side-view action stages and 19 tactical boards, 18 objective families, a DOM-free 20 ms fixed-step side-view action kernel with explicit tactical rollback (`campaign.html?legacyBattle=1`), 14-authority cross-page persistence and signed recovery envelopes, and an optional 215-activity completionist ledger.
- **Playable visual and audio baseline:** complete deterministic pixel-art runtime suites (`party-animation-suite-v2`, `boss-animation-suite-v2`, `kurozane-boss-clips-v2`, `enemy-animation-suite-v4`, `world-tileset-suite-v1`, `field-prop-suite-v1`, `scene-backdrop-suite`, `action-stage-backdrop-suite-v1`, `action-objective-suite-v1`, 16-role `npc-field-suite`, 26-icon item suite, and 9-family battle VFX), paired with opt-in HD/vector candidate lanes (`?art=hd` painted/source-derived character and boss cels, 144-cel six-boss HD presentation VFX, and 62-identity Gothic SVG motion studies), 9 procedural Web Audio score/ambience oscillator families, and a 27-line synthetic Kokoro/Chatterbox EN/JA voice prototype for *The Last Command*.

Per Holleman's *Contractors/Staff* guidance, staffing at this stage must **not** fund speculative brainstorming, late rewrites of the 80% locked design, or generalist studio resumes unfamiliar with party-based 2D RPG production. Every remaining hire or contractor engagement closes an explicit, bounded gap between **implemented deterministic engineering** and **human-reviewed commercial release quality**:

1. **Hire for direct RPG and 2D craft experience over brand names:** prioritize specialists who have shipped 2D action/party JRPG combat readability, modular tilemaps, multi-phase boss animation, historical Japanese localization, and screen-reader-accessible UI.
2. **Match authority to the exact role:** direction roles (Art Direction, Voice Direction, Systems/Narrative Production) are separated from high-volume execution roles (in-between cel articulation, tileset/prop dressing, playtest session coordination) rather than assuming an execution contractor can self-direct style or scope.
3. **Pay professional milestone rates against frozen briefs:** no unpaid/equity dependency, no open-ended hourly drift, and no moving goalposts. Contractors receive locked scene/character/stage specs, SHA-256 intake manifests, and objective technical validators so they see a finishing game rather than endless churn.
4. **Enforce strict provenance and consent:** zero unlicensed voice cloning, zero unattributed scraping, and zero silent promotion of AI/synthetic or comparison-poster placeholders into shipping masters.

### 2.2 Retained Engineering, Tooling, and Narrative Support

Because the custom browser/ES-module runtime, DOM-free action and tactical combat kernels, Storyworld compiler, 14-authority persistence layer, deterministic Python/Pillow/SVG asset pipelines, Playwright browser probes, and `jrpg-verifiers-v2` DAG are already operational and covered by automated tests, **no greenfield engine programmer or external level-editor team is hired**. Technical engineering remains under the Lead Systems Producer / Technical Director as a bounded **15–20% iteration and contractor-integration lane**:

- exposing opt-in asset/audio manifest hooks (`?art=hd`, `drawMap()` prop dressing, streamed OGG music/voice toggles, reduced-horror fixture switches) without breaking deterministic fallbacks or save compatibility;
- tuning numeric combat/pacing levers (`ACTION_BOSS_SHARED_COOLDOWN_FLOOR_MS`, tell poise bonuses, or scene-operation breaks inside the five longest dialogue beats identified in [`docs/40`](40-top-to-bottom-game-evaluation-and-balance-pass.md)) strictly in response to observed human playtest cohorts;
- re-running full-route browser probes and static-delivery checks after every contractor asset drop.

## 4. Review Gates and Acceptance Mechanics

Every contractor deliverable must pass **both** an automated repository integrity check and an explicit human domain sign-off before payment milestone closure or runtime default promotion:

1. **Character & Boss Animation Gate (`Role 2`):**
   - *Automated:* `python game/tools/import-hd-boss-pack.py <pack.zip> --check`, `assets/art/hd-missing-enemies-v1/verify.py`, and `node --test game/tests/roster-keystone.test.mjs game/tests/hd-boss-art-intakes.test.mjs game/tests/hd-boss-final-four-intake.test.mjs game/tests/hd-kurozane-phase.test.mjs` verify 2304×384 / 192×192 geometry, decoded RGBA frame SHA-256 hashes, `< 90 MiB` per-batch Git hygiene, intact pixel fallbacks, and zero simulation mutation.
   - *Human:* Native-scale inspection in `game/hd-source-derived-review.html`, `game/svg-motion-studio.html`, `game/illustration-review.html`, and live `action-campaign-battle.html?art=hd` verifying clean alpha mattes, grounded foot pivots, silhouette distinction across wind-up/active/recovery bands, non-obscuring boss VFX (`game/hd-boss-vfx-review.html`), and period garment fidelity before flipping `visualApproval` in `roster.json`.
2. **Environment, Tileset & Panorama Gate (`Role 3`):**
   - *Automated:* Builder `--check` byte-identity, exact dimension gates on `#mapCanvas`, zero walkability/collision drift across all 48 levels, and lower-48 calm-band contrast checks on `MAP-CIN-001-020`.
   - *Human:* In-engine 320×180 and 2×/4× scaled readability review across rain/ash/fog weather states, objective-token legibility, and cultural sign-off on shrine/chapel/registry and reduced-horror Kurohana processional variants.
3. **Voice & Localization Gate (`Role 4`):**
   - *Automated:* `node --test game/tests/voice-review.test.mjs` and `python game/tools/voice-review-browser-smoke.py` validate manifest schema, pause timings, stale-take lockouts, and zero console/HTTP errors.
   - *Human:* Signed actor consent/licensing ledger (zero voice cloning), native Japanese director approval of every line's register/pronunciation (`余` -> `私`, plain `はい`, clerical vs. colloquial forms) in `game/voice-lab.html`, and ecclesiastical Latin pronunciation verification.
4. **Music & Sound Design Gate (`Role 5`):**
   - *Automated:* Static delivery verification (`python game/tools/static-delivery.py`), seamless loop-boundary check, gesture-gated playback compliance, and fallback to procedural Web Audio synthesis if an asset fails to decode.
   - *Human:* Multi-hour listening fatigue review across exploration, Camp archives/councils, and repeat combat circuits, plus verification that wind-up audio cues reinforce visual tell tiers (`docs/43`).
5. **Historical & Cultural Gate (`Role 6`):**
   - *Automated:* `node --test game/tests/historical-cultural-guardrails.test.mjs game/tests/character-display-migration-guardrail.test.mjs`.
   - *Human:* Named external consultant dispositions (`accept` / `revise` + commit recheck) recorded in [`docs/20`](20-historical-cultural-audit.md) and [`docs/24`](24-external-cultural-review-packet.md) for all 11 independent review questions and 5 mandatory asset packages.
6. **Accessibility & Human Playtest Gate (`Role 7`):**
   - *Automated:* `node --test game/tests/accessibility-current-build.test.mjs game/tests/playtest-evidence.test.mjs` verify ARIA contracts and schema-v2/v3 receipt signatures.
   - *Human:* Logged Narrator, NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver test matrices; uncoached blind FP-1 JSON receipts scored by a second reviewer; chapter-by-chapter exit timing logs; and clean-start UUID-bound 5-hour narrative and 20-hour completionist Credits receipts (`durationProven: true`).

### Budget Guardrails (Holleman 80/20 Discipline)

- **20% Iteration Reserve:** Hold an explicit **20% contingency buffer** on top of base contractor milestones solely for playtest-corroborated readability, balance, and cultural-review revisions. Late changes exceeding the 20% iteration boundary (such as adding new regions, changing the combat camera/perspective, or inventing ability-gated backtracking) are prohibited because they trigger the 50%+ late-rework cost multiplier warned against in the project-plan template.
- **Triage Threshold on Playtest Feedback:** Fix deterministic bugs, accessibility blockers, cultural-audit findings, and low-cost clarity/typo issues immediately; only redesign gameplay or pacing elements when a pattern recurs across a meaningful share of the testing cohort (e.g., >=15–25% of testers stalling on the same tell or objective), rather than chasing 1-to-4-tester subjective preferences that flip other players' satisfaction.
- **Strict Non-Promotion Rule:** Completing and verifying `production.staffing` (`ready` -> `verified`) confirms that roles, contractor sequence, ownership, budgets, and acceptance gates are concretely mapped. It **never** promotes `qa.final-playtests` or `qa.release-readiness` out of `partial`; only real human playtest receipts, assistive-technology testing, and named external cultural sign-off can close those release gates.
